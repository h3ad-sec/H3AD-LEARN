HOW TO USE THIS

GET THE MOST OUT OF IT

01
ANSWER BEFORE YOU EXPAND
Read the question, say your answer out loud or write it down, then expand the model answer. Comparing your own reasoning against it teaches more than reading passively.
02
FILTER BY WHERE YOU'RE WEAK
Open the Workspace and use the domain and level filters to drill into what actually comes up in your interviews, instead of reading the whole bank front to back.
03
READ WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE
Every answer includes a line on what actually separates a strong candidate from a weak one in that specific question. That's usually more useful than the answer itself.
